## Notes

### Tutor behavior — answers to open questions

- **Should the tutor give the final arithmetic result when that calculation is just one step of a larger problem?** No — have the student do the calculation and then confirm it. This is already the current behavior.
- **Should the tutor provide the relevant formula when a student is stuck?** Don't lead with the formula. If the student is putting in genuine effort, provide more help. Also differentiate by pattern: a student stuck once vs. a student stuck on every problem should be handled differently.
- **Should the tutor point students to the exact lectures where they can find help?** Current behavior is good — when the student asks which content to revisit, it points to a specific lecture; when the student is lost, it prompts with thinking questions instead.
- **Is the real issue the _kind_ of help the tutor gives, or how hard it is to get formulation help out of it?** Still open — the harder friction is getting formulation help out of the tutor.

### Testing feedback — Claude (Sonnet 5)

- Would not answer graded questions even after the student copy-pasted them — guardrail is working as intended.
- Provides very clear guidelines even when the student is very confused.
- Much clearer context than GPT and the conversation feels very natural.
- Citation structure is looking good; gave good citations.
- Liked that asking for "help with a practice problem" returned all existing practice-problem names and asked which one the student needed.
- Liked that it pointed to a specific week's video and described what to look for in it.
- When given a GA spreadsheet, it correctly said the spreadsheet didn't match any of the practice problems.
- Couldn't provide a clickable link to the referenced content (told the student to navigate the course site instead).
- Pasting a data table came through as an image and didn't register — the tutor said the data didn't go through and images aren't preferred. Input handling for pasted tables needs work.
- On PP5, the "double-check your decision-variable table headers" guidance didn't fully make sense to the tester.
- Confused about what the "Key Concepts" document is — the tutor should recognize and be able to cite it.
- Course-content connection is fine but could be better: for Week 7 (optimization using spreadsheet) the tutor cites "optimization using Python." The tutor should assume spreadsheet over Python at all times unless told otherwise.
- For graded assignments, it should direct students to course staff via email (not to the discussion forum).
- Optimization with Solver is needed for good help — the tutor can't read Solver from a paste, so it should clearly state that students using Solver need to share it as a screenshot (this is where most of the issues occur).
- Bug: intermittently returns "I could not generate a valid response. Please restate your last message in one or two sentences so I can help."

### Testing feedback — GPT

- Liked GPT's initial framing instructions — even without listing every problem option, it clearly guided the student to identify the problem and share a first attempt even if messy.
- Tone felt very harsh and too firm (e.g., "I'm not going to audit the whole spreadsheet," "I'm not finding the lesson for you"). CTAs have raised the same.
- Even without checking Solver, the initial guidelines were better than what Claude gave before seeing the Solver.
- Sends students to Python first, which could be scary for learners. First suggestion should be lecture content, then recitations with spreadsheet/Excel as the default preference — Python content is optional.
- Held the boundary against inspecting the spreadsheet even when the student pushed, which felt unhelpful in context.

## Decisions

- Tutor should confirm student-computed intermediate results rather than producing them itself.
- Tutor should not lead with formulas; escalate help based on genuine effort and whether the student is repeatedly stuck.
- Default all course-content references to spreadsheet/Excel over Python unless told otherwise.
- Route graded-assignment questions to course staff email, not the forum.
- Tutor must tell Solver users to share a screenshot, since it cannot read Solver from a paste.
