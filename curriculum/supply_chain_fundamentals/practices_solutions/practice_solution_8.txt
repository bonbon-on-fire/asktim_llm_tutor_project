Practice Problem 6: Continuous vs. Periodic Review 2

Continuous vs. Periodic Review
Which of the following are acceptable reasons to prefer a Periodic Review system to a Continuous Review System?
Select the answer that matches
discussion forum below. Your post will be indexed in the right category and it will be easier for the staff to answer it!
More predictable workload due to better coordination across items

Lower reviewing costs / lower reviewing errors

Lower Safety Stock for the same LOS
Explanation
A Continuous Review policy provides tighter inventory control and better service than a Periodic Review policy. However,
there are valid reasons to prefer a Periodic Review policy.
Under Periodic Review policies, you have the opportunity to synchronize the ordering across multiple items. They also
provide a more predictable workload for the people managing these items, and makes for much greater ease of
management and lower review costs.
Also, for fast moving items especially, we are more likely to make an error in inventory accounting when there are
multiple transactions as in the case of Continuous Review.
However, Continuous Review policies have a lower time period (only L) over which protection from Safety Stock is
required than Periodic Review Systems (L plus R), and therefore require less Safety Stock to provide the same LOS.
