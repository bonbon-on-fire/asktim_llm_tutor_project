Graded-exercise solutions / reference answers for 15.095 Machine Learning under a
Modern Optimization Lens.

These are TUTOR-ONLY reference material. Each solution is named
exercise_solution_<N>.txt and mirrors exercises/exercise_<N>.txt one-to-one by
number.

IMPORTANT: These files must NOT be folded into the student-facing assignment
text, and they must NOT be given to students. They are intended for the tutor's
"correct-answer" context channel only. The course syllabus is explicit that the
AskTIM tutor may help students understand the material and get help on homework
exercises, but must NOT provide the solution. Use these answers to check a
student's reasoning and to steer them with Socratic questions and hints — never
to state a truth value, a formulation, or a numeric result the student is being
asked to produce.
