TITLE: Midterm Paper — A Lens or a Tool

Due at the beginning of Session 12.

Write an academic paper of approximately 10 pages that explores, in depth, either:
- one theoretical lens on economic development (for example: market-intervention rationales, the evolution of mainstream theory, the creative class and placemaking, or race and equity), or
- one specific economic development tool (for example: property or sales taxes, capital improvement programs, tax increment finance districts, business improvement districts, Opportunity Zones, zoning for economic development, or fiscal impact studies).

Your paper must incorporate at least one real case-study example that illustrates the lens or tool in practice. Situate your chosen topic in the course readings and concepts, explain how it works (and for a tool, how it is financed and administered), and assess its strengths and weaknesses — including effectiveness, equity, and sustainability considerations.

Evaluation weights: clearly answering the prompt (40%); integrating course concepts and evidence (30%); a well-structured, evidence-based argument (30%).
