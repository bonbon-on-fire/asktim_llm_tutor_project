Practice Problem 1: Mulcreavy Artisans

Part 1 — Correct answer: Financing their inventory; Offering a dynamic (invoice) discount
Explanation: Option 1: False. There is no guarantee that MA would get much return for invoices from customers with such erratic buying processes.
        Option 2: False. While this is clearly an option, there are drawbacks because their customers do not seem reliable and their buying processes quite unpredictable.
        Option 3: True. This is one of the two choices because MA needs cash in order to build inventory prior to events.
        Option 4: False. While this is a good thing to do and will probably get results in the long term, this will not immediately address their needs.
        Option 5: True. This option plays directly into the customer’s willingness to pay with incentive.

Explanation: Option 1: False. This would not provide necessary incentive because this offers a discount only AFTER the 2-day post event window closes.

        Option 4: False. This would not provide necessary incentive because this offers a discount only AFTER the 2-day post event window closes.

        Option 2: True.

        Option 3: False. Cretan is opting for the 7% discount, not the 3% discount, and the payment period is 1 day not 5 days.

        Option 4: True.
