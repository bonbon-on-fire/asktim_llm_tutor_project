Course description

15.071 The AI Edge is a practical introduction to the foundations of modern AI, spanning predictive machine learning, generative AI, and agentic AI systems. The course has three objectives: understanding the technologies underlying modern AI, building AI-powered products with AI as a coding companion, and identifying and leading opportunities to create value through AI. It begins with the core methods of supervised learning — regularized regression, classification, tree-based methods, and recommendation systems — before progressing to neural networks, transformers, computer vision, multimodal AI, large language models, LLM applications, and AI agents. Throughout, students build hands-on solutions in Python using modern AI libraries and connect technical advances to the managerial questions of deployment, evaluation, and governance. The course follows a problem-driven approach organized around business case studies across marketing, finance, healthcare, energy, transportation, supply chains, sports analytics, manufacturing, and digital platforms, teaching students not only how to understand the foundations of the AI revolution but also how to identify opportunities in AI, build AI-powered applications, and lead AI initiatives within organizations.
