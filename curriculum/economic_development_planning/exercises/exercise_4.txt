TITLE: Final Project — Case Study of a Real Economic Development Effort

Final project due at the beginning of Session 23; final presentations in the concluding sessions.

For your capstone, produce an original case study of approximately 15-20 pages examining a real economic development effort — a project, program, district, or policy pursued by an actual locality.

Requirements:
- Direct community engagement is required. You must go beyond online research: talk to the people involved (planners, developers, officials, residents, business owners, or advocates) and bring their perspectives into your analysis.
- Evaluate the effort's effectiveness using both a holistic framework (goals, equity, community outcomes, sustainability, unintended consequences) and a financial framework (public costs, revenues or value captured, incentives used, and who bears the costs and reaps the benefits).
- Connect your findings back to the course's lenses and tools: which rationale motivated the intervention, which instruments were used, and what the case teaches about when and how local government should act.
- Deliver a final presentation summarizing your case and its lessons.

Evaluation weights: clearly answering the prompt (40%); integrating course concepts and community insights (30%); a well-structured, evidence-based argument (30%).
