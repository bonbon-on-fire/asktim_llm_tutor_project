Graded-exercise solutions / reference answers for 15.071 The AI Edge.

These are TUTOR-ONLY reference material. Each solution is named
exercise_solution_<N>.txt and mirrors exercises/exercise_<N>.txt one-to-one by
number.

IMPORTANT: These files must NOT be folded into the student-facing assignment
text. They are intended for the tutor's "correct-answer" context channel only.
Leaking them into exercise prompt text would defeat the tutor's Socratic design.

NOTE ON HOMEWORK 1: There is no single correct answer to HW1 — the assignment
asks each student to add their own fourth artifact and stress-test it. The
reference file exercise_solution_1.txt holds five worked solutions provided with
the assignment. Per the course's own note, these are here to calibrate grading
and to seed discussion after submissions are in; they are deliberately spread so
that no two share a reader, a tool, or a kind of value, and no student is
expected to produce something at that length. Use them to recognize the shape of
a strong answer (a tightly specified artifact, a reader who was being ignored, an
honest account of what moved across runs) — not as a key to hand out.
