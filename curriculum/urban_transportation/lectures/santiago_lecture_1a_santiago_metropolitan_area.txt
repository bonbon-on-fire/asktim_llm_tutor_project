The RM
- Six Provinces, 52 Municipalities
- Smallest of the nation’s 13 regions in size, but home to 40% of population
- Province of Santiago (32 comunas), Greater Santiago (34 comunas), the SMA (?? comunas)
- 90% of RM population in the 34 comunas of Greater Santiago

The RM Topography and Meteorology
- 2,800 km2 Basin – 500 m above sea level
  - 80 kms N-S by 30 kms E-W
  - surrounded on E by the Andes (pre-cordillera peaks of 3,200 m), on W by Coastal Range (cordillera de la Costa with peaks of 2,000 m), with smaller ranges to the North and South
  - Mapocho River runs directly through Santiago, the Maipo runs just south of the SMA.
- Mediterranean Climate
  - Warm, dry, breezy in summer; cold winter, with infrequent storms.
- Thermal Inversion
  - Persists throughout the year
  - Exacerbated in fall and winter

Economy of the RM
- Fundamental, continuously growing role in Chile’s economy
  - Despite intentions over the years at decentralizing growth
- 1986-1996
  - RM economy AAGR: 7.9%; Nation: 6.8%
  - RM share of GDP: 42% Þ 47%
  - RM share of industrial activity: 44% Þ 52%
  - RM share of construction activity: 37% Þ 41%
  - RM share of commercial activity: 65% Þ 68%
- All banks and 46 of 47 of major economic groups HQ’ed in RM
- National center of education (universities)
