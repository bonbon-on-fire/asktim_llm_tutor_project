TITLE: Professional Memo #2 — Value Capture and Tax Increment Finance in Toledo

Due at the beginning of Session 17.

Continuing in your role as Deputy Mayor for Planning & Economic Development for Toledo, Mayor Kapszukiewicz now wants concrete financing ideas for a specific development opportunity.

Write a 1-2 page professional memo (addressed to the Mayor) that develops concrete ideas for using Value Capture and/or Tax Increment Finance (TIF) districts to fund economic development in Toledo. Your memo should:
1. Explain the mechanism you are proposing (value capture and/or TIF) and why it fits the situation.
2. Include at least one quick numerical example estimating how much you might collect in funds — state your assumptions (e.g. assessed value, incremental value, tax rate, capture period) — and describe how you would use the proceeds.
3. Note the main risks or equity concerns and how you would manage them.

Keep it practical and decision-oriented, in the voice of a professional advising an elected official. Graded on a check-plus / check / check-minus basis.
