TITLE: Professional Memo #1 — Getting Started on Economic Development in Toledo

Due at the beginning of Session 7.

You are the newly hired Deputy Mayor for Planning & Economic Development for Toledo, Ohio, working for Mayor Wade Kapszukiewicz. The Mayor has asked you for your initial thinking on how the city should approach economic development planning.

Write a 1-2 page professional memo (addressed to the Mayor) that:
1. Describes one or more ways to view the issue — i.e., which lens(es) from the course you would bring to Toledo's situation (e.g. mainstream/market-intervention, creative class and placemaking, equity and social justice, Jacobs-style import replacement) and why.
2. Offers a few concrete ideas to get started exploring — first questions to ask, data to gather, or early directions worth investigating.

This is a framing memo, not a finished plan: the goal is to show that you can size up the problem through the course's conceptual lenses and translate them into first practical steps. Write in the voice of a professional advising an elected official. Graded on a check-plus / check / check-minus basis.
