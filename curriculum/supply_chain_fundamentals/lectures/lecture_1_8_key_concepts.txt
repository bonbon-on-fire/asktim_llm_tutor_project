Week 1, Lesson 2 · Video 1: Key Concepts

In this lesson, we'll introduce some of the key concepts that we're going to use throughout the remainder of this course, and in fact, you'll probably end up using a lot of these concepts throughout your entire supply chain management career. So specifically, we're going to talk about three big items. First, we're going to talk about Push versus Pull Systems. This is just a way to categorize different systems, and these can be inventory systems, forecasting, supply chains-- it doesn't matter-- any kind of system as to whether it's push or pull. Then we'll move on to Segmentation Strategies. Segmentation Strategies are one of the key tools that you will need to help you run a supply chain. We're going to talk about different ways you can segment your different products as well as segment the different types of supply chains that you have within your firm or within your organization, because segmentation is the way that you'll be able to customize the processes that you have to fit the products, the customers, and the suppliers that you're working with. And finally, we'll wrap up this lecture with handling uncertainty. We'll just talk about different ways that we will work with uncertainty and variability in demand and Lead Time and all those other things.
