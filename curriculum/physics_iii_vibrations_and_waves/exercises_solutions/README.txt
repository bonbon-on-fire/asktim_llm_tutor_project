Reference solutions for MIT 8.03SC Physics III: Vibrations and Waves.

These are TUTOR-ONLY reference material, transcribed from the solution PDFs
published on MIT OpenCourseWare. Each solution is named
exercise_solution_<N>.txt and mirrors exercises/exercise_<N>.txt one-to-one by
number.

IMPORTANT: These files must NOT be folded into the student-facing assignment
text. They are intended for the tutor's "correct-answer" context channel only,
and they are excluded from the RAG index so a solution is never surfaced by
similarity search. Leaking them into exercise prompt text would defeat the
tutor's Socratic design.

COVERAGE (read this before assuming a solution is missing):

  exercise_1 .. exercise_10   Problem Sets 1-10   NO SOLUTIONS EXIST.
      OCW publishes the 8.03SC problem sets without solutions - the syllabus
      says so explicitly ("Problem Sets (no solutions)"). This is a property of
      the source, not a gap in our ingestion; do not go looking for them.

  exercise_11 .. exercise_15  Practice exams      SOLUTIONS PRESENT.
      Practice Exam 1, Practice Exam 2, and Practice Final Exams 1-3 each ship
      a full worked solution PDF, transcribed here.

  exercise_16, exercise_17    Exam 1, Exam 2      NO SOLUTIONS PUBLISHED.
      The real Fall 2016 exam papers are posted; their answer keys are not.

The Fall 2016 final exam paper itself is not published either - only its formula
sheet and the three practice finals.
