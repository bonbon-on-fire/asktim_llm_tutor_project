Fundamental Principle III: Service Integration
- Integrated Transport Network (RIT — Rede Integrada de Transporte)
  - Transfer stations and terminals link busway services
  - Interdistrict (circumferential) routes introduced
  - Fare integration across feeder and interdistrict services

Fundamental Principle IV:
Ongoing (Affordable) Innovation
By 1990: “Victim of its own success”
- Articulated Buses on busways
  - 170 pass./bus
  - 40 second headways –
- 1991: Express (“Direct”) Buses (Linha Directa)
  - Boarding tubes; limited-stop buses
  - Pre-board payment; same-level boarding
  - 10,000–14,000 pass./lane/hour

Express (“Direct”) Buses
- 110 Passenger capacity
- 3.2 times as many passengers per hour as standard bus route
- US$200,000 per km to build
- 9,000 passengers per direction per hour

1992: Bi-articulated Buses
Bi-articulated buses introduced on busways
- 270 passenger capacity per vehicle
- Built for Curitiba at local Volvo factory
- Utilizing Tube Stops
- Lower-cost alternative to Light Rail
