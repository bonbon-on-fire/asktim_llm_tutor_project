vehicles shaped as a bean pod. The Pods deliver small packages around the city, providing full traceability and accurate
delivery-time information.
The city of Madrid (Spain) has offered you a contract to provide logistics services in the city center. They believe the Pods

pooling location.

Madrid city center. You have collected the following information:

loading and unloading the van).
